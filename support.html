Frequently asked questions

A try-on failed. Did I lose a credit?
No. Failed generations refund the credit automatically.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Haircut AI Pro → Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the current period.

I bought credits but they didn't appear.
Go to Settings → Restore purchases in the app. If that doesn't help, email us with the date of purchase.

How do I delete my photos?
Press and hold a look in History to delete it, or use Settings → Delete my data to remove everything.

Where are my stickers?
In Messages, tap + → More → Haircut AI. For WhatsApp, create 3 stickers and tap "Add to WhatsApp".
