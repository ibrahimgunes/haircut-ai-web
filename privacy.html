Privacy Policy

App: Haircut AI: Try On Hairstyles · Developer: FLZ Software Solutions · Contact: support@bridestudio.app
Effective date: October 28, 2026

Haircut AI ("the app") is developed and operated by FLZ Software Solutions ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, where it is processed, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it.

In short: your photo is used only to create the looks you ask for. We do not sell data, show ads, or use your photos to train AI models. You can delete any result, or all of your data, inside the app at any time.

1. What we collect

  • Your photo. When you try a look, the photo you choose is uploaded so a new hairstyle can be generated. For stickers, a copy of your result with the background removed on your device is uploaded.
  • Generated images. Your results, stylist card views and stickers are stored so you can see them in History.
  • An anonymous account. The app signs you in anonymously. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • A device identifier stored on your device, used only to give the free welcome credit once per device.
  • A notification token, if you allow notifications, used only to tell you when a look is ready.
  • Purchase information. When you buy credits or a subscription, Apple sends us a signed record of the transaction (product, price, currency, transaction ID, expiry) so we can add your credits. We never see your payment details.
  • Messages you send us through "Contact support" or the "Did you like it?" feedback card.

We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

2. Face analysis happens on your device

The Face Analysis feature, the "For you" suggestions, face-centred cropping, the estimate of whether a photo shows a woman or a man (used only to pick which styles to suggest) and background removal for stickers all run on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework and an on-device model. These measurements are not uploaded or stored by us. We do not use face data to identify you and we do not use it for biometric authentication.

3. How photos are processed

Uploaded photos are stored in Google Firebase (Google Cloud) and sent to our own image-generation servers (GPU servers rented from RunPod) running an open-source AI model. They are not sent to third-party AI services and are not used to train any model. For WhatsApp sticker packs, your stickers are sent to our server only to convert them to the WebP format WhatsApp requires; they are not stored.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Your uploaded photo and generated images are kept in your private storage so History can show before/after, until you delete them.
  • If a generation fails, the uploaded photo is deleted immediately and your credit is refunded.
  • Delete a single look by pressing and holding it in History (or the trash button on its page). This removes the image and its original photo from our servers.
  • Settings → Delete my data removes all of your photos, generated images and history from our servers immediately. Purchased credits are kept.
  • Images you saved to your Photos library, stickers added to Messages or WhatsApp, and anything you shared are on your device or with the services you chose, and are not affected.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your data. It is processed only by our infrastructure providers — Google Firebase (storage, database, functions, notifications), RunPod (image generation) and Apple (purchases) — to run the app, or disclosed if required by law.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Only your own account can read your images and history. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

7. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to object to processing. You can delete your data directly in the app; for any other request, email us.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; important changes will be announced in the app.
